# License FAQ

### Can you summarize what Companion Even Realities's license allows?

The license grants free rights to use, modify, and redistribute Companion Even Realities source code for personal or nonprofit purposes. The intent is to stay permissive for those uses while limiting commercial abuse of Companion Intelligence products.

Companion Even Realities is a pluggable local-agent platform for Even Realities G2 smart glasses: an Even Hub app (`ci-glasses-app/`) that puts a voice-first heads-up display on the G2 lenses, paired with a local `agent-hub` service that exposes one OpenAI-compatible wire in front of Hermes (Ollama), OpenClaw, Claude Code, and Codex.

Companion Even Realities uses the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0). The full text is in [`LICENSE.md`](../LICENSE.md).

### What freedoms do you have as a Companion Even Realities user?

If you use Companion Even Realities for personal or nonprofit purposes, you can view, modify, and redistribute the source under the same license terms, subject to the PolyForm Noncommercial restrictions.

### Can I fork Companion Even Realities?

Yes. For noncommercial use, you can fork, modify, and redistribute Companion Even Realities under the same license.

### Can I contribute to Companion Even Realities?

Yes. Open a pull request on this repository.

### Can I sell Companion Even Realities?

No. Selling Companion Even Realities, including cloud-hosted instances, appliances that ship it, or paid support for those products, is not permitted. For commercial partnership questions, email partner@companionintelligence.com.

### Does this license apply to the rest of Companion Intelligence?

Companion Portal stays a separate commercial product — this license does not apply to it.

Companion Hub, Companion Memory, Companion Marketplace, Companion OS, Companion Capture, Companion Browser Extension, Companion OMI, Companion Mentra, Companion Pebble, and Companion Home Assistant Voice PE use the same PolyForm Noncommercial terms in their own repositories.

Companion Pipe and Companion MaixCAM are still under legal review for third-party licensing terms in code they bundle — don't assume they're covered by this FAQ until that review lands.
